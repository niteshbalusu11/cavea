# Drawing a seating chart

This guide is for the person who knows the room: a box-office manager, an events coordinator, a venue's operations team. It takes you from a floor plan to a seating chart that buyers choose seats from, in the order you would work. You need no technical knowledge, only a computer with a mouse or trackpad, a browser window at least 1,280 pixels wide (on a smaller screen, zoom the page out), and the floor plan of your room.

By the end you will have drawn your rows, numbered them the way your tickets print them, given each seat its price level, checked the chart and published it, so that your box office can put events on sale with it.

## Words this guide uses

- *Chart*: the drawing of your room: where every seat is, what it is called, and which price level it belongs to. You draw it once and use it for many events.
- *Category*: a group of seats sold at one price level, such as "Stalls", "Circle" or "Restricted view". The chart says which seats belong to which category; the price of each category is set for each event, outside the chart.
- *Publishing*: making the chart, as it is now, available for events. Each time you publish, the chart gets a new *version* number. An event is always created from one version and keeps it.
- *Hold*: a seat a buyer has chosen but not paid for yet. You do not deal with holds in the designer; they happen on the buyer's page.

9. [Preview and publish](publishing.md): what buyers will see, versions, and events.

## What the designer cannot do yet

So you do not look for them:

- *Tables, booths and standing areas.* The designer draws rows of seats only. A banquet room can be drawn as short rows; a standing area cannot be sold from a chart yet.
- *Several floors.* A chart has one floor. A venue with a balcony is drawn on one plan, the balcony beside or behind the stalls, in sections of its own.
- *Grouping drawings together.* Shapes and text cannot be grouped; seats are grouped into sections (see [Arrange the chart](arranging.md#sections)).
- *Importing from other tools.* Charts from other seating systems, CAD drawings and spreadsheets cannot be read. Trace a picture of the plan instead ([Start a chart](start.md#trace-a-floor-plan)).
- *Text on the chart.* The **Text** tool does nothing yet, so the stage cannot be named on the chart: draw it as a shape.
- *The view from the seats (the focal point)* on a chart started in the console: see [Check the chart](checking.md#no-focal-point).
