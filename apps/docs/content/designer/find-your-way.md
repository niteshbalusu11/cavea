# Find your way around

This page names the parts of the designer's screen and shows how to move around a chart, so the rest of the guide can say "the inspector" or "the status bar" and you know where to look.

![The designer: tools on the left, the canvas in the middle, the inspector and panels on the right, the status bar at the bottom](../images/designer-screen.webp)

## The parts of the screen

| Part | Where | What it is for |
|---|---|---|
| Top bar | Along the top | The **Menu**, the chart's name, undo and redo, whether your work is saved, **Preview** and **Publish** |
| Tools | Down the left | What a click or a drag on the canvas does: **Select**, **Hand**, **Row block**, **Arc block**, **Single row**, the shapes, **Section**, **Category brush**, **Focal point**, **Image tool**. Hover over a tool to see its name and its key |
| Canvas | The middle | Your chart. The rulers along its top and left edge measure it in chart units |
| **Inspector** | Top right | Everything about what is selected: its position, its size, its category. With nothing selected, the chart's own settings |
| Panels | Bottom right | Three tabs: **Layers** (everything on the chart, in a list), **Categories** and **Validation** |
| Status bar | Along the bottom | How many seats and objects the chart has, how many are selected, the problems found, **Snap**, **Grid** and the zoom |

The inspector and the panels can each be folded away with the arrow at their top right (**Collapse Inspector**, **Collapse Panels**), and made wider or taller by dragging the line between them.

## Move around the chart

- Scroll with the mouse wheel or two fingers on a trackpad to move up and down; hold `Shift` to move sideways.
- Zoom by holding `Ctrl` (`⌘` on a Mac) while you scroll, or by pinching on a trackpad. The zoom follows the pointer: point at what you want to look at closely.
- Drag the view with the **Hand** tool (`H`), by holding the space bar while you drag, or with the middle mouse button.
- **Zoom to fit**, the percentage at the bottom right, shows the whole chart. `Ctrl`+`0` does the same.
- **Zoom to selection**, `Ctrl`+`2`, fills the screen with what is selected.
- **Zoom in** and **Zoom out** are the `+` and `−` beside the percentage.

## Light and dark

Open the **Menu** at the top left, choose **Theme**, then **Light**, **Dark** or **System** (the same as your computer). Press `Escape` to close the menu. The theme changes only how the designer looks to you; buyers' maps follow their own device.

## Undo and redo

Every change can be undone, in order, with the arrows in the top bar or `Ctrl`+`Z`, and redone with `Ctrl`+`Shift`+`Z`. Hovering over the undo arrow says what it would undo ("Undo Seat pitch"). Numbering a block, however many seats it changes, is one step. The history lasts until you close the chart; it is not kept when you open the chart again later.

## Commands and shortcuts

Every action has a name in the command palette: press `Ctrl`+`K`, type a few letters of what you want ("align", "insert", "tour") and press `Enter`. Press `?` for the list of keyboard shortcuts, or see the [keyboard shortcuts](shortcuts.md) page.
